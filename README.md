# Privacy Policy - CryoShard Shield Runner

**Last Updated:** October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile application **CryoShard: Shield Runner** (herein referred to as "the Application").

## 1. Information Collection and Use
The Application is designed as an offline casual arcade game. We do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personally identifiable information (PII) or sensitive personal data from users.

## 2. Local Data Storage
Game preferences, local high scores, crystal counts, and setting toggles (such as haptic feedback preferences) are stored locally on your device. This data remains strictly on your device and is not uploaded to external servers.

## 3. Device Permissions
The Application may request device vibration/haptic permissions solely to provide physical feedback during gameplay interactions. No camera, location, contacts, or internal storage access permissions are required or requested.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The Application does not utilize third-party tracking services, analytics SDKs, or targeted advertising frameworks that collect user information.

## 5. Children's Privacy
The Application does not address or target anyone under the age of 13, nor does it knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will be published directly on this page.
